## Level 01 – Description

Level 01 is a survival-based arena game in which the player must collect coins while avoiding enemies.

### Game Objects

- **Green Ball:** Represents the Player, controlled by the user.
- **Red Balls:** Three red balls represent Enemies. They move around the arena and can damage the player.
- **Coins:** Coins spawn at random positions throughout the arena. They are distributed across the playing area.

### Gameplay

1. The player controls the green ball and moves around the arena.
2. Three red enemy balls patrol the arena and try to collide with the player.
3. When the player collides with an enemy, the player loses health and is pushed backwards.
4. Coins spawn randomly around the arena, and the player must collect them to increase the score.
5. After collecting a coin, another coin spawns at a different position.
6. The target score is **10 points**.

### Win and Lose Conditions

- **Win:** The player wins after reaching the target score of 10 points.
- **Lose:** If the player's lives or health run out before reaching the target score, the game ends in defeat.

### Objective

The main objective of Level 01 is to survive enemy attacks, avoid collisions, collect coins, and reach the target score of 10 points.
